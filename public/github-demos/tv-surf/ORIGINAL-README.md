# TV Surf

A web experiment that mimics the feeling of 90s television channel surfing, using YouTube videos.

## Concept
Instead of searching and watching videos from the beginning, "TV Surf" drops you into a curated list of channels (Nature, LoFi, Space, Cooking). When you change the channel, you are dropped into a random video at a random timestamp, simulating a live broadcast that is already in progress.

## How to use
- Open `index.html` in your browser.
- Click anywhere to begin (required to allow autoplay).
- Use the **Up/Down arrow keys** or the on-screen buttons to surf channels.

## Inspiration
Inspired by a top Hacker News discussion ("If YouTube had actual channels") where users praised the mechanic of jumping into the midpoint of a video, replicating the serendipity of channel surfing.
