How to Play

You have 18 scrambled words. For each word, tap the letters in the correct order to spell it. You have 10 seconds per word.

🤔 What does this tool do?

Scrambl generates 18 words seeded from today's date. Everyone who plays today gets the exact same words in the exact same order. This lets you compare scores with friends — no accounts needed.

💻 Use cases for Engineers

  • Brain warm-up: A 2-minute cognitive warm-up before deep work sessions to activate pattern recognition.
  • Team stand-up icebreaker: Post your Scrambl score in Slack — the daily shared puzzle creates natural water-cooler conversation.
  • Interview decompression: A quick dopamine reset between intense coding rounds.

📊 Use cases for Product Managers

  • Meeting buffer game: Play while waiting for the last person to join the Zoom call.
  • Team engagement metric: Share the daily Scrambl as a Slack ritual to measure and boost async team engagement.

⚠️ Challenges

  • Some words are genuinely hard to recognize when scrambled — that's the point.
  • The 10-second timer is tight by design. It creates real pressure.
  • Only one puzzle per day — come back tomorrow for a new one.
