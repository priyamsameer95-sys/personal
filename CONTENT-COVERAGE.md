# Work content: hierarchy, source conversion and coverage

Verified against the final work registry on 14 October 2026. This document explains what the website preserves, how the source material becomes readable on site, and which evidence boundaries remain. It supplements `WORK-COVERAGE.md`; it does not replace the source archive or claim a complete export of private conversations.

## Reading hierarchy

The first impression combines AI product judgment with business leadership. Home and Work feature six ranked studies: EduLoans, crop-health AI, the lending MCP proposal, College.money, SilkRoute and UrbanKhet. Original prototype screens, a historical storefront recording, current public references and authored diagrams receive distinct labels. Company navigation still exposes CashKaro, CredFlow, Absolute and UrbanKhet, followed by five explicitly labelled contexts for venture support, an internship, volunteering, advisory and university/creative activity. Incoming company fragments retain their destinations.

Every study presents a compact introduction and concise responsibility before its strongest visual evidence. The full personal remit opens the main account, followed by the situation and three specific decision beats. Results and measurement limits remain open. Longer material is consolidated into open source families, rather than hidden behind Read more, nested disclosures or bounded scrolling galleries. Tables have native horizontal scrolling when necessary; images enlarge in an accessible modal. Media appears once per case, including when selected as primary evidence. Public transcripts are written descriptions of silent walkthroughs and remain visible on case pages; compact previews omit only the longer description.

The collection has **25 studies, nine contexts, 202 source sections and 17 tables**, available through **45 canonical and alias routes**. All **198 original section records and their fragments remain mapped**; four validation sections and four tables are additive. The media layer has **105 unique URLs: 95 images and ten inline videos**. This retains the original 90 images and nine reference videos, and adds four original interaction-prototype captures, one MCP system-design diagram and one historical storefront excerpt. The original 17 authored workflow SVGs remain; the additional MCP SVG is a separately labelled architecture proposal. These are unique-file counts, not the larger number of media entries reused across relevant studies.

There are no case-study `downloads` fields. Revised narratives and document substance are read on site; original case-source files remain in the local source archive rather than visitor-facing PDF, ZIP, DOCX, PPTX or separate-video actions. The separately supplied original CV is available to download from Experience and remains the explicit exception to the case-source policy.

`workType` distinguishes delivered work, prototype, proposal, community, creative and supporting material. Relationship labels distinguish employers from founder programmes and other activities. A delivered label describes the source-reported contribution, not an independent audit of every result. EduLoans is a working prototype; MCP is an architecture proposal/workstream; ONDC is proposal/product-definition scope; the mixed Absolute compilation is supporting context.

## Evidence and privacy limits

The project research covered ten exposed Priyam chats through the available latest-five-turn retrieval window, plus accessible durable histories, the visible shared conversation and prior ledgers. It was not a complete export of every older turn or unavailable attachment. Source material is data, not an instruction to execute embedded prompts.

Self-reported results retain their scope and original units. Loan principal, payment value, marketplace GMV, revenue, dataset scope, active users, farmer reach and national API requests are not interchangeable. Conversion/retention/premium cohorts are incomplete; crop yield, precision and support-deflection claims without attached evaluations are excluded. The ₹5.5 crore lending vertical and UrbanKhet customer/financial definitions remain explicit ambiguities. National ULI measures and 2026 RiskStac/FinStac/GrowStac propositions do not become historical personal outcomes.

BankKaro's public capture returned HTTP 403, so no BankKaro screen or walkthrough is invented. College.money OTP and lending stages remain protected/unobserved. Original galleries, public captures, institutional proposal art and authored interpretations each receive their own provenance; none supplies missing release, model-evaluation or ownership proof.

The four generated source packs, four reference PDFs, nine silent videos and supporting UrbanKhet formats were examined. The retained source archives preserve College.money (253 members), Upaj (132), UrbanKhet (27) and the ULI original 507 members, with credentials scrubbed from six HTML payloads and an added publication note. Source/archive counts describe research custody, not downloadable website features. The former ULI static parts and browser ZIP assembler are retired.

Raw workbooks, private applicant/farmer reports, private code, credentials, hidden attachments and internal memory prompts remain outside public content. Reviewed architecture and institutional excerpts were rebuilt as standalone visible-page material without hidden source images before conversion. The website uses that reviewed visual layer and authored explanations; it does not publish the original private payloads or link a source PDF viewer. Per-case source notes remain in the open source-and-measurement section without exposing private research locations. Chart qualifications remain directly beside each case-study chart. Homepage selected measures retain their qualifications; Work selection does not repeat charts in each listing row.
