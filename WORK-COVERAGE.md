## Attribution and measurement limits

Public pages captured in October 2026 are current product references. They do not establish ownership of later branding, artwork, every current capability or authenticated operations. RiskStac, FinStac and GrowStac are visible reference sections alongside the historical risk, insurance and farm-product work. The ULI material documents national infrastructure context, not a claim that Priyam built or owned that entire platform.

BankKaro returned HTTP 403 during capture. There is no invented BankKaro screenshot or walkthrough. College.money's production attribution comes from the shared conversation; inaccessible OTP-protected stages were not represented as directly observed screens.

Business figures are source-reported, not independently audited. Each outcome retains its measure and scope: loan principal is not platform revenue, payment volume is not fee income, GMV is not revenue, and a dataset count is not predictive accuracy. In particular:

- The ₹5.5 crore monthly disbursement figure has education-specific and wider-lending descriptions. It remains CashKaro lending context rather than a study-abroad-only headline.
- UrbanKhet's ₹1.2 crore figure is variously labelled P&L scope and ARR, so it is omitted as a headline financial outcome. Its waste comparison uses an asserted industry benchmark, not a company before-and-after baseline.
- ULI borrower-versus-loan counts and the under-30-minute timing window are not consistently defined. Partner lending outcomes remain separate from personal platform ownership.
- Crop-health yield, precision and support-deflection claims lack attached evaluations; unsupported causal outcomes are excluded. Rainfall-premium, retention and funnel measures retain their source notes where cohorts or definitions are missing.
- Projections, targets, working prototypes and open backlog items remain distinct from completed releases. Additional Absolute compilation figures have unresolved personal attribution and appear as supporting context.

## Publication exclusions and privacy

Raw career workbooks, private research reports, applicant and farmer records, original private code repositories, credentials and internal memory prompts are withheld. Their supported product contribution is represented through narrative, scoped outcomes and reviewed excerpts rather than publication of the raw files.

The six-page EduLoans architecture excerpt and three-page Absolute institutional excerpt were rebuilt from reviewed visible pages before source conversion. Those retained reference PDFs contain only the rendered page images, with no hidden source images, source attachments or annotations. The website uses reviewed images and authored explanations rather than embedding or linking those PDFs.

Reviewed galleries, inline videos and on-site reference chapters are the public evidence layer. Per-study `sourceNotes` preserve material limitations without exposing the private research ledger or local source locations.
