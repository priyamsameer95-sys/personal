## Research coverage

- **Priyam project conversations:** ten exposed chats were retrieved using the available latest-five-turn window. Accessible durable histories and earlier source ledgers supplemented those windows. This was not a complete export of every project conversation, hidden attachment or older turn; unavailable material is not presented as reviewed.
- **Career archive:** all 87 product-story records and 49 leadership records were reviewed, for 136 records in total. Of these, 134 are mapped to the grouped studies. Two memory-only records are held out because a supported experience account was unavailable. The archive's metric register, platform-depth records, timeline, source register and unresolved questions were also used to reconcile scope.
- **Career and technical documents:** the Professional Profile, dated CV/resume versions, AI project materials, lender-policy and workflow documentation, and additional Absolute notes informed the narrative. A draft question or incomplete interview prompt is not treated as a completed incident. No coaching, handover or model-evaluation episode was invented to fill those gaps.
- **Shared portfolio conversation and generated packs:** the visible shared conversation, four generated source packs and their member inventories were reviewed. These supply current public product references and historical venture material; a capture or generated interpretation is distinct from evidence of personal delivery.
- **Additional local documents:** institutional presentations, relevant architecture material and the MoneyLion strategy/retention variants were examined. Duplicate versions remain versions, rather than additional employment or separate delivered programmes.

## Attribution and measurement limits

Public pages captured in October 2026 are current product references. They do not establish ownership of later branding, artwork, every current capability or authenticated operations. RiskStac, FinStac and GrowStac are visible reference sections alongside the historical risk, insurance and farm-product work. The ULI material documents national infrastructure context, not a claim that Priyam built or owned that entire platform.

BankKaro returned HTTP 403 during capture. There is no invented BankKaro screenshot or walkthrough. College.money's production attribution comes from the shared conversation; inaccessible OTP-protected stages were not represented as directly observed screens.

Business figures are source-reported, not independently audited. Each outcome retains its measure and scope: loan principal is not platform revenue, payment volume is not fee income, GMV is not revenue, and a dataset count is not predictive accuracy. In particular:

- The ₹5.5 crore monthly disbursement figure has education-specific and wider-lending descriptions. It remains CashKaro lending context rather than a study-abroad-only headline.
- UrbanKhet's ₹1.2 crore figure is variously labelled P&L scope and ARR, so it is omitted as a headline financial outcome. Its waste comparison uses an asserted industry benchmark, not a company before-and-after baseline.
- ULI borrower-versus-loan counts and the under-30-minute timing window are not consistently defined. Partner lending outcomes remain separate from personal platform ownership.
- Crop-health yield, precision and support-deflection claims lack attached evaluations; unsupported causal outcomes are excluded. Rainfall-premium, retention and funnel measures retain their source notes where cohorts or definitions are missing.
- Projections, targets, working prototypes and open backlog items remain distinct from completed releases. Additional Absolute compilation figures have unresolved personal attribution and appear as supporting context.
- MoneyLion is an independent authored exercise, not employment or a commissioned engagement. Its pilot targets, budgets, cohort curves and financial scenarios are explicitly assumptions, not observed MoneyLion results.

## Publication exclusions and privacy

Raw career workbooks, private research reports, applicant and farmer records, original private code repositories, credentials and internal memory prompts are withheld. Their supported product contribution is represented through narrative, scoped outcomes and reviewed excerpts rather than publication of the raw files.

The six-page EduLoans architecture excerpt and three-page Absolute institutional excerpt were rebuilt as standalone PDFs from reviewed visible pages. Their published files contain only those rendered page images, with no retained hidden source images, source attachments or annotations. This prevents omitted report pages or private image objects from travelling inside an apparently safe excerpt.

Reviewed galleries and publication-safe downloads are the public evidence layer. Per-study `sourceNotes` preserve material limitations without exposing the private research ledger or local source locations.
