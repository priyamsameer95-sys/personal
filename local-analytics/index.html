A few limits worth knowing.

Visitors are anonymous. Visitor-days estimate browsers within each day, then add those daily counts. A returning visitor may count on more than one day. Browsing is never linked to a CV email.

Clicks record navigation, project and company links, CV actions, contact choices, media and filters. No entered text, raw IP addresses, full browsing URLs or personal contact destinations are collected. Do Not Track, Global Privacy Control, bots and signed-in website administrators are excluded. Previous traffic cannot be reconstructed.
