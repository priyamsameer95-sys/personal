How to read this dashboard

Unique visitors count browser identifiers, not verified people. Different devices, private windows and cleared cookies can count separately. Sessions end after 30 minutes without measured activity. Sankey bands count tab journeys so parallel tabs cannot create false navigation.

Active time is measured while a page is visible and focused, pausing after 60 seconds idle. Lost final updates can leave timing incomplete. Older visits cannot be reconstructed into journeys or reading time.

Emails are never joined to browsing histories. No entered text, raw IP addresses, full browsing URLs, screen recordings or personal contact destinations are collected. Do Not Track, Global Privacy Control, known bots and owner exclusions are respected. Raw traffic records are retained for 180 days.
