# Work, Experience and Art: one predictable portfolio

Preserved baseline: branch `codex/before-work-experience-art-2026-10-16`, commit `5fd4474`. Implementation branch: `codex/work-experience-art`. The accepted homepage opening stays intact.

## Reader's task

Work answers what Priyam built, for whom, the decision he owned and what evidence supports it. Experience answers where, when and with what responsibility. Art lets someone encounter the actual paintings and their original notes. A reader should not reconstruct the company relationships from interleaved themes or guess which website an action opens.

## Contract

- Work uses company sections in this order: CashKaro, Absolute, UrbanKhet. Themes label projects within their company, rather than breaking companies apart. Company context precedes personal remit; directly linked company names replace ambiguous “Company website” actions. UrbanKhet has no external company website link.
- A manual thumbnail strip provides an immediate visual overview and native links into the open project accounts. Next/previous controls scroll that strip; they do not autoplay or hide the main content. Keyboard users can use ordinary links. Controls show their available/disabled state. Motion respects reduced-motion preferences.
- Eight selected projects retain a consistent title/status, visible media, consequential decision, personal contribution and optional full-case action. Actual product destinations are explicitly named: College.money, RiskStac and GrowStac. Upaj/Farm OS supporting previews remain within Absolute.
- Use reviewed existing media only. RiskStac's current public monitoring capture is not historical interface evidence. SilkRoute and lender integration have explanatory diagrams, not invented product screenshots. Prototype, proposal, current-reference and historical distinctions stay adjacent to the visuals.
- Logos use shared, optically balanced slots and retain official colours and aspect ratios. Page layouts align to those shared slots. Do not recolour brand artwork or stretch it into identical shapes.
- Experience uses a consistent CV rail for company identity/dates and an open content column for context, progression, responsibilities and named proof links. Preserve full supported chronology, certificates, CV download and all anchors.
- Art has a thumbnail overview and six distinct original painting entries. Full paintings, notes and details remain open; enlargement uses the existing accessible modal. No invented artist narrative or dates.
- Reuse cream/ink/colour tokens, original assets, shared media, native controls and scoped CSS. Animate small transforms/opacity only, with no autoplay, new animation dependency or blocked content when JavaScript is absent.
