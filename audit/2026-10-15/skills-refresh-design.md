# Design and implementation brief

The portfolio is for a hiring leader assessing Priyam's responsibility, judgment and work. Preserve the supported claims, all case routes and original evidence. Cream paper, black ink and the existing coloured layer remain the identity.

The current homepage repeats its positioning in the introduction, three project notices, technical introduction, help section and closing. It also treats every story as the same ruled text/image row. The first pass will remove repeated explanation, make College.money the lead visual, group the two shorter delivery accounts, and keep prototypes/proposals visibly distinct. A compact career record supplies context; the paintings give the final personal note. The shared footer provides the final invitation.

## Working tokens

- Paper `#faf7f0`; soft paper `#f1ede4`; ink `#171717`; muted text `#625e56`.
- Existing cobalt `#2657ef` and orange `#ff7628` accompany original product imagery. Pink, yellow and lime stay in the underlay and original artwork.
- Geist remains the primary family. Use a clear sentence-case scale rather than new font dependencies, italic emphasis or labels on every block.
- Content stays left aligned, with a controlled reading measure inside wider media layouts. Reserve image dimensions; never conceal essential evidence with entry animations.

## Layout

```text
Identity                    Main pages       CV / Contact

Current remit and place
One clear introduction spanning the available width
Short career context                         Work / CV

Selected work
College.money: responsibility    Large product evidence

Crop-health delivery             UrbanKhet storefront

EduLoans prototype               MCP proposal

Short career record and links to Experience
Bounded ways this experience can help
Painting and artist note
Shared contact footer
```

This avoids a decorative hero object or a large scripted prototype being presented as the person's identity. The distinctive moment comes from the real work and the colours inside it. The coloured underlay becomes a quieter paper edge, rather than a competing frame. Keep a restrained entry sequence and respect reduced motion.
