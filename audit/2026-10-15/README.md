## Reader validation script

Use a recruiter, product hiring leader, founder and peer who did not write the site. Test desktop and phone with the same tasks. Do not coach the answer.

1. After the first screen, describe Priyam's current role and one kind of problem you would bring him.
2. Choose a relevant case from Work. Explain the problem, Priyam's personal responsibility and one consequential decision.
3. Distinguish historical delivery, current public reference, scripted prototype and architecture proposal in the AI/lending examples.
4. Inspect an artifact and explain what it demonstrates. Enlarge it, close it and return to the account.
5. Find the complete chronology and download the CV. Explain any discrepancy you notice.
6. Choose a contact route without sending a message. Explain what you would ask Priyam to discuss.

Record the participant's own words, task completion, wrong turns and the evidence they used. Separate observed problems from taste preferences. A reader study remains pending until actual participants perform these tasks.
