## Source and attribution decisions

- Four EduLoans images were captured from the supplied `V1.03-Chat-eduloans-main.zip` frontend at 1440 × 1000. Original React interfaces and in-memory handlers were retained. Person/contact fixtures were replaced with conspicuously synthetic samples; external scripts/fonts were removed; the local capture server forbids network connections. Captions identify **Original interaction prototype — sample data; scripted responses.** Scoring, OCR, action confirmations and operational figures in those images are scripted examples.
- The original prototype's incomplete interactions remain honestly documented. Its thirteen-step counter is distinguished from the fourteen-turn interaction model in the source account. These captures do not establish working integrations or production readiness.
- The second EduLoans archive records **145 passing assertions on 5 March 2026**. It was not rerun. Mocked dependencies, locally reproduced rule helpers and unit/component scope do not establish deployed rule correctness, model accuracy or complete end-to-end coverage.
- Proposed evaluation tables for EduLoans, lending MCP and crop-health AI separate scenarios, expected behaviour, available evidence and status. They are future evaluation plans, not invented successful test results. They follow task-specific [evaluation guidance](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/evaluation-best-practices) and explicit tool contracts and human control in the [MCP tools specification](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/specification/2025-11-25/server/tools).
- The new MCP diagram is explicitly authored proposal material. Shared evaluation, two rule engines, reviewed feedback and human approval are design boundaries; a deployed server is not claimed.
- Crop-health dataset scale is distinct from model accuracy. Current GrowStac references are dated and distinguished from Priyam's historical delivery. RiskStac product evidence precedes national ULI background.
- The two UrbanKhet MP4 inputs were byte-identical. The original 72.56-second recording shows mobile storefront/subscription/cart/checkout and a desktop B2B console. Checkout/contact and staff/account details make the complete recording unsuitable for this public page. One reviewed, silent 20-second storefront excerpt is published, preserving picture/timing and excluding those later segments. The original remains private. Its date is unspecified; old prices and operational notices are historical. Studio Sorted campaign illustrations retain attribution.
- Programme GMV is not revenue; dataset counts are not accuracy; company/team outcomes are not automatically individual causal impact. Prototypes, proposals, delivered work, independent exercises and supporting contexts retain distinct labels.

## Skills and review method

This pass applies the installed **Structure & Pitch** and **Marketing Strategy & PMM** skills to hiring leaders evaluating product judgment and business ownership. The practical sequence is interest → responsibility → consequential decision → evidence and limits → conversation. The earlier audit's broad skill inventory remains a separate private research record; this implementation uses the relevant guidance rather than claiming that unrelated skills informed it.

Independent reviews checked public disclosure removal, source/media retention, reading length, chronology, attribution and mobile qualification sizes. Browser review covers Home, Work, original prototype captures, cases, Experience, Art and Writing; keyboard checks cover media and navigation.
